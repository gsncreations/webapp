GSN CREATIONS — HUMAN-FIRST CONTENT WORKFLOW

There is no published Google AdSense rule that limits a site to a particular percentage of AI-written content.
The safer approach is to publish useful, original, people-first material based on the real projects.

For every new project:
- Start from the actual hardware and measurements.
- Upload real project/circuit/wiring images.
- Record the real connections.
- Describe real problems and fixes.
- Do not invent test results, specifications or personal experiences.
- Use the assistant for editing, structure, clarity and formatting, not for fabricating the project.

For blog posts:
- Base the article on a real build, test, tutorial or lesson.
- Add original photos, diagrams, code and observations when appropriate.
- Keep downloads secondary to the useful explanation.
- Review every technical statement before publishing.

AdSense approval is never guaranteed by a template or by an AI/human percentage.

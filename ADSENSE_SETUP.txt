GSN CREATIONS — ADSENSE SETUP

This website is structured to be AdSense-ready, but no site can be guaranteed approval by code alone. Google reviews the live site and its content.

1. Publish the site on your real domain. Do not submit localhost or the Live Server address.
2. Make sure Home, Projects, individual project pages, Blog, About, Contact, Privacy Policy, Terms and Disclaimer are accessible.
3. In AdSense, add your real domain under Sites and complete Google's verification/review process.
4. After Google gives you your publisher ID (ca-pub-...), open assets/js/adsense-config.js and replace the empty client value.
5. Deploy again. The site will then load Google's official AdSense script.
6. In AdSense > Privacy & messaging, configure the Google CMP / required consent messages for applicable regions.
7. When AdSense provides your exact ads.txt seller line, copy that exact line into a new root-level ads.txt file. Do not use the example line.
8. If you use Auto ads, enable them in AdSense. The website does not invent ad slots or fake ad IDs.

Important content strategy
- Keep project pages genuinely useful and detailed.
- Keep blog articles original and specific to your own builds.
- Keep navigation clear.
- Do not place ads beside or inside download/navigation controls.
- Do not ask visitors to click ads.
- Do not publish thin pages made only for downloads.
- Keep Privacy Policy accurate to the services you actually use.

Before submitting to AdSense
- Replace placeholder site/domain text.
- Check every navigation link.
- Test mobile layout.
- Test every project download and every animation download.
- Check that no old GIF/BIN tool links remain.
- Add your real contact details.
- Add your real project photos and circuit diagrams.

GSN CREATIONS - Solar Tracking Prototype

PROJECT OVERVIEW
A cardboard-friendly solar tracking prototype that turns a panel toward stronger light.

USED COMPONENTS
- Small solar panel
- Microcontroller
- Light sensors
- Servo(s)
- Breadboard
- Jumper wires
- Cardboard mechanical frame

CONNECTIONS
- Light sensors → analog inputs selected by the sketch
- Servo signal → configured PWM-capable control pin
- Servo power → suitable external supply where required
- Controller, sensors and servo grounds → common ground

WORKING PROCESS
1. Measure light on multiple sides
2. Compare sensor readings
3. Move the servo toward the brighter side
4. Repeat at a controlled interval
5. Stop or center when readings are close

SCOPE / WHAT YOU LEARN
- Solar-energy demonstrations
- Feedback/control loops
- Sensor comparison
- Servo control
- Cardboard prototyping

IMPORTANT NOTE
Keep the panel and servo mechanically supported and use a suitable supply for the servo.
